Copista's read-aloud voice: notices
===================================

The voice is optional. Nothing below is downloaded until you set it up in the Auditorium.


1. The model: Supertonic 3
--------------------------

Copyright (c) 2026 Supertone Inc.

The voice is a modified copy of Supertonic 3 by Supertone Inc.
(https://huggingface.co/supertone-oss-archive/supertonic-3), taken at commit
aafc6e32416a594460b32413efc49d7fe4ce6d46 and published at
https://huggingface.co/GiuseppeSPk/copista-voice.

What was changed: in onnx/duration_predictor.onnx, onnx/text_encoder.onnx,
onnx/vector_estimator.onnx and onnx/vocoder.onnx, every float32 weight larger than 4 KB is
stored as float16, and a Cast node turns it back into float32 when the model loads, so all
computation stays in float32. Nothing else in the graphs was changed. onnx/tts.json,
onnx/unicode_indexer.json, voice_styles/M2.json and voice_styles/F1.json are unchanged
copies.

The model and this copy are released under the BigScience OpenRAIL-M licence, including the
use-based restrictions in its paragraph 5 and Attachment A. The full text is in
openrail-m.txt, next to this file.

This copy is not an official release and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Supertone Inc.
